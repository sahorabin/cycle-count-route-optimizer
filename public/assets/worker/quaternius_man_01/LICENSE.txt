Modifications made for this project (permitted under CC0, listed for honesty):
  - The source is a rigged GLB with 11 animation clips. One frame of its "Idle"
    clip (t = 0.4s) was baked into a static mesh: skinning was applied per
    vertex, normals were recomputed for the posed geometry, and the skeleton,
    skin, and all animation data were dropped. The app therefore loads a posed
    mesh with no rig and runs no animation mixer.
  - The leg triangles of the "Skin" primitive (centroid below y = 1.62 in model
    units) were partitioned into a separate "Workwear" primitive so the renderer
    can dress them as work trousers. Geometry was partitioned, never deformed:
    the vertex positions are unchanged and the triangle count is identical.
  - Material colours in the file are the author's originals. PPE colouring
    (hi-vis top, dark workwear, dark boots) is applied at render time by
    material name and is not baked into this file.
