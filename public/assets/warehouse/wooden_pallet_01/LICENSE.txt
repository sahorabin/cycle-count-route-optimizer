Modifications made for this project (permitted under CC0):
  - The "WoodenPallet" object from the pack's Crates.zip was converted from
    Wavefront OBJ to glTF 2.0 (quads triangulated, vertices de-duplicated,
    texture V axis flipped for glTF UV orientation). Geometry is unchanged.
  - The 1024x1024 PNG diffuse map was resized to 512x512 and re-encoded as
    JPEG for web delivery. The pack's normal and specular maps are not used.
